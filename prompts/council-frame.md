- title: a short, plain name for the idea (no hype).
- kind: what sort of idea it is in 1–3 words (e.g. "Business idea", "App feature",

- restated: the idea in your own words, sharpened — what it is, who it is for,
  what success looks like. Fill obvious gaps sensibly and say what you assumed.
- assumptions: the beliefs it silently depends on. The ones that, if wrong,
  sink it. Be specific ("people will pay ~$10/month", not "there is demand").
- sections: the 3–6 angles THIS idea should be judged on. Pick them for the
  idea — a business needs market and economics, a technical idea needs
  feasibility and architecture, a life decision needs trade-offs and
  reversibility. Each has a short snake_case key, a heading, and a focus line
  telling the members what to examine.
- questions: 3–5 research questions the web can actually answer (prior art,
  competitors, prices, data, regulations, known failures). Concrete and
  searchable.
