Spot-the-bug rules (the player taps the one broken line):
- 6–12 lines of realistic code in one language that illustrates the concept.
  One entry per line, keep indentation, max 80 characters per line.
- Exactly ONE line is wrong, and it is wrong because of the concept being
  taught (not a typo or syntax error). Every other line must be correct.
- "bug_line" is the 0-based index of that line. "fix" is the corrected line.
- "why": 1–2 plain sentences on why it's wrong.
