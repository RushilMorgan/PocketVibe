Someone is exploring an idea and keeps a numbered list of frameworks they have
learned. Choose the ones worth working this idea through.

Pick at most 3, best first. A framework earns a place only if applying it would
show something specific about this idea: a risk, a gap, a choice to make.
Prefer ones that look at the idea from different angles over three that say the
same thing. If none really fits, return an empty list; a forced fit wastes
their time.

Return JSON: "picks" is the numbers from the list.
