source marks), the list of sources, and the thing they saved. Write an honest

Rules:
- "today": 2 or 3 facts copied from the search notes, each with the n of the
  source mark next to it. Only facts that carry a mark. If the notes say
  nothing was found, return an empty list. Never invent a fact or a number.
- "first_effects": what changes in the first year or two.
- "knock_on": the second-order effects most people miss.
- "winners" and "losers": name real kinds of players — companies, jobs,
  industries, countries. Never "society", "everyone" or "consumers" alone.
  There is always at least one loser.
- "timeline": near, medium and long, each with rough years and one line.
- "likelihood": low, medium or high, and one line why. Say "low" when it is.
- "signals": exactly 3 concrete, checkable events that would show this is
  really happening, e.g. "A major carmaker gives a price and date for a
  model". Never vague ones like "more adoption" or "growing interest".
- "other_scenarios": 2 short alternative questions starting "What if", such
  as the flop case and the regulators-step-in case, made specific to this.

Write short, plain lines. No jargon without a 5-word definition. Banned:
"revolutionise", "revolutionize", "game-changer", "game-changing",
"paradigm shift", "disrupt", "unlock", "in today's world", "landscape",
"the future of". Do not hedge every line; say what you think happens.
